# Horns Board v1.0
**Board Requirements**

## Overview and Description
- The primary function of this board is to act as a solid-state power switch that drives two vehicle horns wired in series using a 24V bus.
- This board provides an isolated, fused switching circuit to replace direct manual wiring or bulky mechanical relays.
- It receives power from the Low Voltage Bus Board and an On/Off switching signal from the Dash Board.
- Other board integration:
	- Low Voltage Bus Board (Power: +24V)
	- Dash Board (Digital GPIO Control)
- Wiki page: [Electrical Onboarding Fall 2026](https://wiki.illinisolarcar.com/w/index.php/Public:Electrical_Onboarding_Fall_2026)

## High-Level Requirements
- Microcontroller
	- None. This board operates entirely with discrete components.
- Functional Features
	- Horn On/Off: Must accept a digital GPIO signal (3.3V/5V logic) to control horn activation.
	- Horn Output: Must deliver +24V at 150mA across two horn loads connected in series.
	- Inductive Kickback Protection: Must safely clamp back-EMF voltage spikes generated by the horn coils when switched off.
	- Overcurrent Protection: Must include an inline fuse rated for 2A to protect upstream power distribution.
	- Noise Rejection: Must filter the digital input signal to prevent accidental horn activation from chassis or high-voltage EMI.

## Communication Protocols
- Digital GPIO (Single-wire active-high switching signal referenced to common ground).
	- No external protocol transceiver ICs required.
	- Signal line filtered via RC low-pass filter prior to driving the switching gate.

## Connectors
- Connectors must be polarized and rated for at least 1A DC.
- Power In (1x3 Pin Connector):
	- Pin 1: GND
	- Pin 2: +24V
	- Pin 3: GND
	*(Symmetrical ground arrangement prevents reverse polarity damage if connected backward)*
- Horn Control (1x2 Pin Connector):
	- Pin 1: HORN_CTL (Digital input from Dash)
	- Pin 2: GND
- Horn Out 1 (1x2 Pin Connector):
	- Pin 1: +24V (Fused supply)
	- Pin 2: Series Interconnect Pin
- Horn Out 2 (1x2 Pin Connector):
	- Pin 1: Series Interconnect Pin
	- Pin 2: HORN_IN (Switched path to MOSFET drain)

## ICs
- None.

## Buttons/Switches
- Optional tactile push button wired in parallel with the gate signal to test/debug the horn circuit directly on the bench without a connected Dash Board.

## Power System
- +24V DC Rail:
	- Originates from the Low Voltage Bus Board.
	- Protected by an onboard 2A fuse.
	- Bulk filtering consisting of eight parallel capacitors: 3x 0.1uF, 2x 1uF, 2x 10uF ceramic capacitors, and 1x 100uF polarized radial capacitor.

## Test Points
- +24V (Raw power in)
- +24V_FUSED (Downstream of 2A fuse)
- HORN_CTL (Input gate signal)
- HORN_IN (MOSFET drain / low side of series horn load)
- GND (Ground reference)

## LED Indicators
- +24V Power Indicator: Green LED indicating fused 24V supply is active.
- Horn Active Indicator: Yellow/Red LED showing when the MOSFET gate is driven high.
